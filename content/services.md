## House Call Grooming

A.D.A.P.T. comes to you. Every appointment is one-on-one; just your dog, in their own space, with a groomer who takes the time to do it right.

No crates. No cages. No other animals. No chaos. Just calm, attentive care from start to finish.

---

### What's Included

Every appointment is all-inclusive:

- Bath and blow dry
- Breed-appropriate haircut or trim
- Nail trim
- Ear cleaning
- Paw pad care
