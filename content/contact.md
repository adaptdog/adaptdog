### What to Expect

When you reach out, it helps to have a few things ready:

- Your dog's breed, age, and approximate weight
- Their coat condition and when they were last groomed
- Any behavioral notes: nervous, reactive, sensitive areas, etc.
- Your general availability

The more I know upfront, the better I can prepare to give your dog exactly what they need.
